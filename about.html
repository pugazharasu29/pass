ABOUT DIGI BUS PASS

The DIGI BUS PASS is an innovative system designed to streamline the process of bus travel for both conductors and passengers. Our goal is to make public transportation more efficient, convenient, and accessible for everyone. With DIGI BUS PASS, passengers can easily manage their travel passes, while conductors can effortlessly verify and manage passenger information. We are committed to improving the public transportation experience and making it as seamless as possible. Thank you for choosing DIGI BUS PASS!
